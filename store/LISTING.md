================================================================================
FULL DESCRIPTION
================================================================================
SwiftBlock is a free Chrome extension that hides Taylor Swift–related mentions from the pages you browse.

It works like KardBlock (James Shamsi, 2015), which filtered Kardashian–Jenner content the same way: scan the page for known names and aliases, then hide the surrounding feed card, article, or list item—not the whole page.

What you get
• On/off toggle and an editable keyword list
• Sensible defaults (names, aliases, Eras Tour, Taylor’s Version, distinctive song titles)
• Works on typical news, feed, and YouTube-style layouts as the page updates
• Settings stay in your Chrome sync storage

Privacy
SwiftBlock does not track you, does not sell data, and does not phone home. There are no accounts and no analytics. Filtering runs locally in your browser.

Open source (MIT). Install, tweak keywords in the popup, and keep scrolling.
