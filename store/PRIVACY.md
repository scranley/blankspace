# SwiftBlock Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-18

SwiftBlock is a free Chrome extension that hides Taylor Swift–related mentions on web pages using local keyword filtering.

## What we collect

SwiftBlock does **not** collect, sell, share, or transmit your browsing history, personal information, or any other user data to the developers or to third parties.

## What stays on your device

Your settings (on/off toggle and keyword list) are stored only in Chrome’s sync storage on your Google account / browser profile (`chrome.storage.sync`). That data never leaves Chrome’s storage APIs for this extension’s purposes.

## How the extension works

A content script runs on pages you visit (http and https), looks for your configured keywords in page text, and hides matching feed or article elements. All filtering happens locally in your browser.

## Permissions

- **storage** — save your preferences and keywords.
- **Host access (http/https pages)** — allow the content script to filter those pages.
